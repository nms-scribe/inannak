The World of Inannak

Forest Elven

Forest Elven is a colloquial dialect spoken among the upper Elven castes outside of the strict legal and religious institutions of the Elven Empire.

Old Elven is known from records to be almost 10,000 years old, as old as the founding of the Elven Empire. But records are found of a language called Ælvesytsirwywǝ (/alvɛsɪt͜siɹɪwə/ "Forest Elf Tongue") emerging among the Forest Elves as early as 3,000 years after that. The language persisted despite numerous attempts at reform. Only a couple of thousand years ago, the Council agreed to support this dialect in less formal venues. Today it has become the language of commerce and daily chores among all of the upper Elven castes, when it is not necessary to speak in Old Elven.

This language differs greatly from that of Old Elven. While it shares many familiar words, their pronunciations can be quite different. While lexicons exist, as well as attempts to standardize spelling, the language is not as regulated as Old Elven, and several dialects and variations of pronunciation are found throughout the Elven world.

Grammar

The grammar of Forest Elven is much different from Old Elven. It is an inflective language, with a system of about 12 cases. Word order is generally not important, with speakers choosing to place in front the subject, object or even verb depending on what's more important in context. Nouns are divided into two classes, animate and inanimate, which are based on their actual features -- although nouns may be reclassed for poetic or metaphorical reasons.

Phonology

Forest Elven does not have the broad/slender distinctions that Old Elven does, nor does it retain the aspects of vowel harmony. Nevertheless, it has a similar number of consonants, and even more vowels. However, the complex diphthongs of Old Elven are gone. Vowels in hiatus are pronounced individually with at most a small voiced glottal approximant between them.
